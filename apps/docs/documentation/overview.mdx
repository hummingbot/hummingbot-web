Hummingbot is an open source framework for building, deploying, and running
algorithmic trading bots and AI agents across 50+ centralized and decentralized
exchanges. It's self-hosted — your keys, your server, your strategies.

<Note>
  These docs are being migrated from the legacy site at hummingbot.org/docs.
  For Condor (the AI agent harness), see the **Condor** tab. For connector
  details, see the **Exchanges** tab.
</Note>
